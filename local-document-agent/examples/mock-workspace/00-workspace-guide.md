# Harbor mock workspace

This entire collection is fictional. Names, organizations, budgets, dates, and
email addresses are sample data, not real people or business records.
Snapshot date: 20 November 2026. All meeting times are UTC.

## Project documents
- projects/harbor/overview.md: project scope and proposed launch.
- projects/harbor/meeting-2026-11-18.md: earlier decisions, including a proposed review date.
- projects/harbor/meeting-2026-11-20.md: current decisions and confirmed review date.
- projects/harbor/action-items.txt: owners, due dates, dependencies, and status.
- projects/harbor/risks.md: known project risks and mitigation owners.

## Finance and people

- finance/harbor-budget-2026-11-20.txt: current approved budget and proposed spending.
- finance/expense-policy.md: fictional approval rules.
- people/team-directory.md: fictional roles and example.invalid email addresses.

## Working documents
- policies/document-handling.md: fictional project record-keeping conventions.
- operations/weekly-status-template.md: a sample report layout.
- operations/onboarding-checklist.txt: fictional participant onboarding steps.
- knowledge/harbor-faq.md: frequently asked project questions and source pointers.

## Plain-text email

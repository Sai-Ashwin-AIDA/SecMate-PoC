# Harbor document-handling conventions

Fictional project conventions. Version 1, dated 20 November 2026.

- Dated historical records are retained rather than silently rewritten.
- A superseded proposal should not be presented as a current approval.
- Reports distinguish confirmed facts, proposals, risks, and open decisions.
- Budget summaries identify currency, document date, and conditional amounts.
- Meeting summaries include owners and due dates when those facts are recorded.
- Missing facts remain explicitly unknown; an absent record is not evidence of completion.
- Email drafts are review material, not evidence that a message was delivered.

The current Harbor budget is identified by its explicit CURRENT label and date.
The 18 November budget remains useful for explaining what changed.

These conventions describe the fictional project's records. They do not grant
additional application tools or access to files outside the selected collection.

# Harbor expense approval policy

Fictional project policy, not real company policy. Effective 20 November 2026.

## Scope
This policy describes approval records for the fictional Harbor pilot.
All amounts are USD excluding tax.

## Approval rules
- Every purchase needs a documented purpose and a quote or estimate.
- Expenses up to USD 250 require written approval from Avery and Casey.
- Expenses above USD 250 also require a separate sponsor sign-off.
- An approved budget envelope is not individual purchase approval.
- A quote, reservation request, or draft email is not a completed purchase.

## Minimum expense record
Record a request ID, date, supplier, purpose, currency, amount, budget category,
approver names, and approval status.

## Workshop example
The proposed USD 240 workshop is within the lower approval tier, but it still
requires both written approvals. It is not committed spending until approved.
